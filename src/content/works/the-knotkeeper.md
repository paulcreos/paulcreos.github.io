## The work

The Knotkeeper is a fictional placeholder for an upcoming circular relief. It holds the idea of a boundary that protects not by closing itself, but by continuing without end.

## Material and making

The future piece will follow the same meeting of CNC preparation, hand carving, pyrography, and oil finish used throughout this evolving body of work.
